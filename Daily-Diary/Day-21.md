# Day 21 – Introduction to TheHive and Security Incident Management

**Training Duration:** 1 Month  
**Day:** 21

## 1. Objective
To understand TheHive, its features and importance in cybersecurity. To learn how TheHive is used for security incident management, alert handling and organizing incident response activities.

## 2. Topics Covered
- Introduction to TheHive
- What is TheHive?
- Importance of Incident Management
- TheHive Architecture
- Introduction to Case Management
- Security Alert Management
- Incident Tracking and Investigation
- TheHive Installation Requirements
- Introduction to Cassandra and Elasticsearch
- Role of TheHive in SOC Operations

## 3. Learning Activities
- Studied the concept and purpose of TheHive.
- Learned how TheHive helps security teams manage incidents.
- Explored the features of TheHive for alert and case management.
- Understood the difference between security alerts and incident cases.
- Studied the role of Cassandra and Elasticsearch in TheHive setup.
- Learned how incident information can be organized and tracked.
- Explored how TheHive can be integrated with Wazuh and Shuffle for automated incident management.

## 4. What I Learned
I learned that TheHive is an open-source security incident response and case management platform. I understood how it helps security analysts organize alerts, create incident cases and track investigation activities. I also learned about the role of Cassandra and Elasticsearch in the TheHive environment and how TheHive works with Wazuh and Shuffle to support automated incident response.

## 5. Tools Explored
- TheHive
- Cassandra
- Elasticsearch
- Wazuh
- Shuffle
- Ubuntu Linux
- Web Browser

## 6. Challenges Faced
Understanding the architecture of TheHive, the purpose of its supporting components and the difference between alert management and case management.

## 7. Conclusion
The twenty-first day of training provided knowledge about TheHive and security incident management. It helped me understand how security incidents are organized, tracked and investigated using a case management platform. This knowledge is important for managing security alerts and improving incident response in a SOC environment.
