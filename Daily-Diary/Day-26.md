## 3. Theoretical Knowledge
VirusTotal is an online threat intelligence platform used to analyze suspicious files, URLs, domains, and IP addresses.

It uses multiple antivirus engines and security tools to identify potentially malicious content. Users can search for indicators such as file hashes, URLs, and IP addresses to check whether they are associated with known threats.

Threat intelligence is the process of collecting and analyzing information about potential cyber threats. It helps SOC analysts understand suspicious activities and make better security decisions.
