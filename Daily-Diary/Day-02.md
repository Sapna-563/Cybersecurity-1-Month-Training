# Day 02 – Introduction to Security Operations Center (SOC)

**Training Duration:** 1 Month  
**Day:** 02

## 1. Objective
To understand the concept of a Security Operations Center (SOC), its importance and the responsibilities of SOC analysts in monitoring and protecting computer systems.

## 2. Topics Covered
- Introduction to Security Operations Center (SOC)
- Importance and Functions of SOC
- Roles and Responsibilities of SOC Analysts
- Different Levels of SOC Analysts (L1, L2 and L3)
- Security Monitoring and Threat Detection
- Incident Response Process
- Introduction to SIEM

## 3. Learning Activities
- Studied the basic structure and working of a Security Operations Center.
- Learned how SOC teams monitor networks and computer systems.
- Understood the responsibilities of SOC Level 1, Level 2 and Level 3 analysts.
- Explored how security alerts are generated and investigated.
- Learned the basic process of identifying, analysing and responding to security incidents.

## 4. What I Learned
I learned that a Security Operations Center is responsible for continuously monitoring an organization's IT infrastructure to identify and respond to cybersecurity threats. I also understood the different roles of SOC analysts and how SIEM tools help collect and analyse security logs to detect suspicious activities.

## 5. Tools Explored
- Wazuh
- Windows Event Viewer
- Kali Linux
- SIEM

## 6. Challenges Faced
Understanding the different levels of SOC analysts, their responsibilities and the process of security incident detection and response.

## 7. Conclusion
The second day of training provided knowledge about SOC operations, security monitoring, SIEM tools and the responsibilities of security analysts in protecting organizational systems from cyber threats.
