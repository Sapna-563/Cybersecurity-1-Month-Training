# Day 01 – Introduction to Cybersecurity

**Training Duration:** 1 Month  
**Day:** 01

## 1. Objective
To understand the fundamentals of cybersecurity and the importance of protecting computer systems, networks and data from cyber threats.

## 2. Topics Covered
- Introduction to Cybersecurity
- Importance of Information Security
- Types of Cyber Threats
- Introduction to Network Security
- Basics of Security Monitoring
- Introduction to Security Operations Center (SOC)

## 3. Learning Activities
- Studied the basic concepts of cybersecurity.
- Learned about different types of cyber attacks.
- Understood the importance of protecting sensitive information.
- Explored the role of security monitoring in organizations.
- Learned the basic responsibilities of a SOC analyst.

## 4. What I Learned
I learned that cybersecurity protects computer systems, networks and information from unauthorized access, attacks and damage. I also understood the importance of identifying security threats and monitoring suspicious activities.

## 5. Tools Explored
- Windows 11
- Kali Linux
- VirtualBox

## 6. Challenges Faced
Understanding different cybersecurity concepts and types of cyber threats.

## 7. Conclusion
The first day of training provided a basic understanding of cybersecurity, its importance and the role of security monitoring in protecting digital systems.
